## Melee root cause and correction

The previous patch replaced the original `func_489` melee driver with
`func_502`. `func_502` steers special movement but bypasses
`func_526 -> global507/global508 -> func_516`, so the existing N-followup
motion had no normal melee forward-speed owner.

Current target `grapparam.bin` parses as 12 rows and contains both
`0x68C7334E` and `0x159D8BC7`. Both have `trackingFrame=180`; changing rows is
not the fix and would also change unrelated move timing. The correction keeps
`0x68C7334E`, restores `func_938 -> func_489`, removes the custom channel-5
seed, and retains only the form-origin latch/air-state preservation.
