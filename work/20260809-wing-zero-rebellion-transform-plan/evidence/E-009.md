## Root causes and changes

1. Slot 0 used independent three-argument bindings. This stage changed ENTER
   and EXIT to reversed unmoded four-argument `sys_4F(0xB)` pairs. E-011 later
   proved that form also copied raw ammo and replaced it with mode `0x4`.
2. Both bird projectiles consumed `global681 == 0`. Projectile 1 remains on the
   real slot; projectile 2 now uses virtual slot `0x5`.
3. Bird melee reused ground `func_937`; `func_489 -> func_516` overwrote forward
   movement while teardown disabled air hold. A form-origin latch now selects a
   `func_502` pursuit adapter, preserves air hold through teardown, clears old
   flight channel `0x8`, and seeds channel `0x5` once. The normal `func_937`
   path is unchanged.
