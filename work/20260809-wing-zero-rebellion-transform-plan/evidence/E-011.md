## Findings

1. Rebellion normal main `0x750B4B8E` is 10 rounds and bird main
   `0x77A3426B` is 2 rounds. Both rows have the same one-stage charge profile.
   The previous four-argument swap copied bird raw ammo back into the normal
   slot, producing 2/10 after exit.
2. Official Delta Plus is the matching reference: normal `0x1486A84F` is
   4 rounds, WR `0x377D1397` is 2 rounds, and both rows have the same charge
   profile. `func_1037` and `func_1038` use a final mode argument `0x4` in
   both directions.
3. TV bird hash `0x2194F05D` is selected with `(0, 1, 0)`, exactly like
   `0x476FAC14`. Rebellion previously used the ground-charge class
   `(1, 1, 0xB)`, explaining the flight stop.
4. Flight updates the body rotation owned by `func_104`; form teardown did not
   clear either `func_104` base rotation or `func_107` offset rotation before
   normal `ACTION_MASK_1000`.
