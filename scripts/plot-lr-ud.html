Bone placement → left_right (x) & up_down (y)

Drag a bone chip onto the plot. Drop position maps to left_right (horizontal) and up_down (vertical). Dropping again for the same bone updates its coordinates. Drag an existing point on the canvas to move it; values snap to a step of 1 (see STEP in script). Copy all result exports code for generate-hex-template.js; left_right in that snippet matches the values shown (left = positive). Placements are stored in localStorage for this origin and persist across refresh; the first visit seeds from embedded defaults in the script. Use Quick mirror to flip a bone or copy the opposite of another bone without dragging.
