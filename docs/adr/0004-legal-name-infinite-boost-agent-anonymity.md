# Name Infinite Boost in full in legal text; keep agents on later-than-OB

Status: accepted (2026-09-01)

Human-facing AcceptableUse and README name the live arcade title in full —
機動戦士ガンダム エクストリームバーサス2 インフィニットブースト / Mobile
Suit Gundam EXTREME VS.2 INFINITE BOOST — with IB, EXVS2IB, and イニブ only
as aliases. The ban is “any LaterThanObRevision still operated as a live
arcade or online service,” not a single abbreviation. Agent rules and
research evidence keep the existing “later than OB” wording and do not treat
InfiniteBoost as a study target.

## Considered options

- Abbreviation-only (“IB”) in legal text — too vague; IB is a community
  short name, not the product
- Never name the live title (descriptive ban only) — weaker for humans and
  lawyers
- Use the full official names in legal/README text; keep agent research
  anonymous (chosen)

## Consequences

- Legal copy must be trilingual and must include the Japanese official
  title, not only English or IB
- version-scope remains: do not commit later-revision addresses, hashes,
  dumps, or executable identities
- Naming InfiniteBoost in AcceptableUse is not permission to open, dump, or
  cite that revision
